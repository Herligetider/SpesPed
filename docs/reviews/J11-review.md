## R1 — P2: Reading/reviewing derived document text requires generation and write permissions

Location: `backend/spesped/contexts/documents/generation.py:87–93`, particularly the `gateway.current_job` call at line 89. Its gateway `_load` requires `ai.<purpose>`; source resolution calls `load_basis` with its default `documents.write` permission at lines 11/31. Line 91 additionally demands `documents.read`, even for the assigned contributor surface.

Once a human selects generated text, `snapshot_status` invokes this lineage path during normal document reads, approval and contribution reads. Consequently, a distinct legitimate source/content reader receives `content=null` and a misleading processing-change warning unless also authorized to generate and write. A reader with AI permission but without document-write permission still fails. A distinct approver with the document review/approval rights but no AI authority cannot approve; a contributor assigned only one section cannot load that section without whole-document/AI/write rights. The late purpose-assignment repair correctly handles the writer's AI assignment, but does not resolve these different consumer identities.

Independent reproduction on SQLite and PostgreSQL: four probes in `var/review-j11-documents/test_independent.py` named `test_independent_reader_does_not_need_generation_or_write`, `test_independent_reader_with_ai_does_not_need_write`, `test_independent_approver_does_not_need_generate`, and `test_independent_contributor_can_review_selected_generated_section`. Each generates through the real loopback gateway, explicitly selects the typed output, and narrows the separately authenticated teacher's permissions. The first also approves the generated-derived revision before the separate reader accesses it. Eight parametrized failures are captured in `independent.txt`.

Required repair: validate exact processing/job identity and current policy/release separately from permission to initiate generation; retain actor-aware native source checks for the consuming operation. A whole-document reader requires document/source-read rights; an approver requires its exact human review/approval authority; an assigned contributor requires contribution scope and sees only assigned sections/cited authorized sources. Do not grant broad write/AI/read authority to make these reads pass, substitute metadata checks for source prose authorization, or assume writer and reviewer are the same actor. Keep model/source recall effective and preserve caller transaction ownership.

## R2 — P1: Annual ITO applicability omits the obligation's reporting period

Location: `backend/spesped/contexts/documents/adapters.py:95–104`, especially `valid` at line 103; consumed by `service.py:168–173` and the finalization gate at line 281.

The adapter checks learner/case, reviewed source/profile binding, rule applicability, document type and rule effective dates. It never binds the report dates to the concrete obligation's `period_key` or another authoritative reporting-period definition, and omits that period from the returned frozen rule manifest. A currently reviewed rule spanning multiple years therefore allows the same period-specific obligation to validate a report for a different year.

Independent real HTTP reproduction on both databases: create and review an annual ITO rule/profile, trigger an obligation for `period_key=2025` with a 2025 deadline, then select that task for a report dated 1–21 October **2026**. Handle the visible evidence gaps without asserting progress, submit and approve. The response is HTTP 200, `state=approved`, rule state `applicable`, and `legal_finalization=reviewed_report`. The expected unresolved/blocked result fails in `test_independent_annual_obligation_period_is_bound`; full evidence is in `independent-lifecycle.txt`. This does not claim the report updates/completes the governance task; the incorrect claim is the report's own applicability/finalization state.

Required repair: bind the selected concrete obligation and report to an explicit authoritative reporting interval or reviewed period relationship. An opaque arbitrary period key or deadline alone is not sufficient to infer those dates. Missing/ambiguous binding must remain unresolved and prevent statutory finalization, while allowing a visibly manual working draft. Freeze that binding and recheck its currency without rewriting the historical report. Verify mismatched, absent, cross-pupil/case and changed bindings, including a correctly bound annual report.

## R3 — P2: Scheduled request completion does not reconcile scope changed during the model call

Location: `backend/spesped/contexts/documents/generation.py:137–151`. `scheduled_binding` is checked before the gateway call, but not before `_request_outcome` records success.

The authenticated owner can pause the schedule while the gateway's provider invocation runs outside its transaction. After the reply, the request is persisted as `succeeded`, with no reason or scope-review marker, and the proposal diff remains available as a usable proposal. Both database probes printed `plan_state=paused`, `request_state=succeeded`, `request_reason=None`, `diff_status=200` in `independent-pause-availability.txt`. The schedule itself is visibly paused. Replaying the processor would now reject the same scope through `scheduled_binding`; the initial completion bypasses that check.

The gateway really completed generation, and its historical completed job should remain recorded. This finding does **not** require cancelling an already-started provider call, erasing that fact, or claim automatic text application, approval or sending. F20 does not explicitly define cancellation of in-flight generation. The narrower issue is inconsistent current request/proposal availability after the schedule's own current binding became unacceptable. Reconcile the original plan/revision/owner/timezone/document/configuration after the call and expose a renewed-review/blocked current request state when appropriate. Preserve stable job identity and make any subsequent manual adoption an explicit human action with current scope. The exact paused-plan adoption policy should remain explicit rather than treating historical job success as proof of current scheduled fulfillment.

Reproduction: `test_independent_pause_during_generation_rechecks_request` uses a hook only at the real loopback provider invocation to call the real schedule pause API, then lets the provider and native gateway finish normally. The probe's expected blocked-current-request assertion fails on both databases. A pre-execution pause is already covered and blocked by the author tests; it does not cover this completion window.

## Explicit policy questions and supported boundaries

- An `ordinary_subject_assessment` document can be approved under a reviewed school template without selecting a native BC09 finalized statement. `test_independent_ordinary_report_without_bc09_is_marked_reviewed` confirms this on both databases. F18 permits reviewed local reports and does not universally prescribe a BC09 statement, so omission alone is not treated as a separate blocker. This is bounded school template/content-approval policy. It does not establish BC09 formal assessment authority, scheme/exemption/grading compliance, or statutory compliance. A deployment promising a formal ordinary assessment must make the exact finalized BC09 statement and its separate professional authority an enforced policy, not rely on the document type name or `legal_finalization` label. The existing native-statement test proves exact selection and recall when selected.
- Snapshot/current reads preserve the saved revision, text, source manifest and cutoff. Late in-period observations create review need without rewriting approved content. Withdrawal/current-rights failure withholds content; template recall removes current finalization; edits/translation create new unapproved revisions. The tests establish these behaviors for the exercised source paths, not every possible source lifecycle event.
- The historical annual fixture exercises two native activated IOP revisions and exact goal applicability instants, actual delivery separately from plans, a real reviewed rule/profile/task/template, and missing professional outcome handling. The fixture deliberately provides human handling of gaps; it does not manufacture progress or prove real legal sufficiency.
- Guardian recipients remain proposals with `disclosure=not_authorized`. `frozen_revision` requires current exact content approval and returns `disclosure_authorized=False`. Actual J12 dispatch/history, recipient packages, portal access, J20/J21 practitioner UI and J26 deployed scheduling/outbox consumption are outside this pin. Full F18/F20 acceptance is consequently **not established**.
